# PBIP v1.1.0 release notes

Tagged `v1.1.0` on 5 November 2026 from `main`; the tagged commit and its checks are listed on the
[GitHub release](https://github.com/rachitst/pbip/releases/tag/v1.1.0). The full list of changes is in
[`CHANGELOG.md`](../../CHANGELOG.md). Requirement-by-requirement evidence is in
[`traceability.md`](../requirements/traceability.md) and the use cases as built are in
[`use-cases.md`](../requirements/use-cases.md).

## In plain language

v1.1.0 finishes what the use-case document asks for.

- **Loans that fit you.** PBIP looks at your balances, income and spending and suggests real loans from its catalogue,
  with how much you could borrow, the monthly payment and why.
- **Investing that respects your risk profile.** Your risk questionnaire now decides which investments are suggested
  and how products compare for you. You can invest in funds (once or monthly), stocks and bonds, see them in your
  portfolio and sell them. Prices are **simulated**; this is a practice bank.
- **Insurance quotes.** Get a simulated quote and apply; nothing is underwritten and no premium is taken.
- **Your app usage, with your consent.** With your permission PBIP measures how you use the app (sessions, products
  you look at) to personalise suggestions. It is off until you turn it on, and turning it off erases what was
  recorded.
- **For staff:** a fraud monitoring dashboard with charts, more exception types for compliance, and behavioural
  profiles and engagement charts for relationship managers.
- **The assistant** can now list insurance plans and answers questions about every feature.

## Upgrade notes

- Two migrations: `0030` (analytics consent, behavioural profiles) and `0031` (investment instruments, holdings,
  orders, SIPs, insurance applications, the `SYS-INVESTMENTS` account and two ledger transaction types).
- `POST /api/v1/loans/check-eligibility` and `/apply` can now refuse on the new spending and balance rules
  (ADR-0056).
- Clickstream events are stored only for customers who have turned analytics on (ADR-0057). The demo seed turns it
  on for Alice, Bob and Carol.
